Hello again! I'm Elena-Alexandra, a dedicated self-learner. My curiosity fuels my learning, and all my projects shows my commitment to make a positive difference in the world through my work.
I am excited to walk you through my work, to demonstrate how I unravel stories hidden within data, and to share my passion for turning raw information into valuable knowledge that aids decision-making. I hope this portfolio gives you a comprehensive understanding of my abilities and the depth of my commitment to the exciting field of data.


Firstly, this portfolio exhibits my work across diverse Data Science domains. I am excited to present these projects to you, each showing my passion for the field.
Beyond the world of Data, in the past few years, I've ventured into building a startup. All aspects of its planning and development are detailed in this portfolio, marking an experience that was truly useful for me, especially in regards to Business Analysis. Here, you'll see all my work in the domain of Business Analysis, propelled by my software engineering master's program that has equipped me with an invaluable toolkit of analytical strategies.
A pivotal project that I'm proud to share is my collaboration with the Romanian Police. In 2023, I embarked on a deep dive into Romania's traffic accident data spanning 2021 and 2022. The aim was to formulate pragmatic, data-informed strategies to reduce the nation's traffic accident occurrences.
