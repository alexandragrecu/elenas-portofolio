Live Green: Recycling Through Gamification

Live Green is an app that aims to revolutionize the way we approach recycling. It empowers individuals and communities to make a positive impact on the environment by adopting sustainable habits and practices. Through the power of gamification, Live Green transforms recycling into an engaging and rewarding experience for users.

With Live Green, we believe that small actions can lead to significant environmental change. By integrating gamification techniques, such as earning points for completing recycling challenges, we inspire users to adopt eco-friendly behaviors and contribute to a greener future.

Modelling an intelligent

parking system
An intelligent parking system for cars/motorcycles was modeled this way: users can arrive at the entrance with their car/motorcycle, and it will be taken over by an automated system that will place it in an appropriate vacant parking spot (car in a car spot, motorcycle in a motorcycle spot).
The user will be given a voucher with useful information for pickup (e.g., a QR code to scan). Upon pickup, a user will present the pickup voucher, the system will calculate the parking fee based on hourly payment (for regular users) or a preferential rate (for those with a subscription), allow card payment, and upon successful payment, automatically retrieve the vehicle to the user, who can then leave the parking area with it.
